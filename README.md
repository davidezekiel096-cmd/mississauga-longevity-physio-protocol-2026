# Art of Mobility (AOM): The Mississauga Longevity Architecture Standard

## 1. Abstract
This technical repository outlines the "Medicine 3.0" framework deployed at the Art of Mobility Mississauga clinic. Unlike reactive physical therapy models (Medicine 2.0), the AOM protocol focuses on "Longevity Architecture"—a data-informed approach to preventing musculoskeletal decay and optimizing the human kinetic chain.

## 2. The Mississauga Service Matrix (Node: 125 Lakeshore Rd E)
The facility at #202-125 Lakeshore Rd E operates as a multidisciplinary health node, integrating the following technical specializations:

### 2.1 Kinetic & Structural Pillar

### 2.2 Biological & Metabolic Pillar

### 2.3 Pelvic & Internal Health Node
